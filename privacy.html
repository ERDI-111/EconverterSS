Privacy Policy

This policy describes how EconverterS handles data.

What EconverterS Does

EconverterS is a media conversion tool. You can upload an audio or video file, or submit a direct HTTP(S) link to a media file, and the service converts it to MP3 or MP4 using FFmpeg on a private server. No accounts or registration are required.

Uploaded Files

  • Files you upload are stored temporarily on the server as anonymous tokens — your filename is never logged.
  • Input files used for conversion are deleted immediately after the conversion job completes or fails.
  • Converted output files are automatically deleted 30 minutes after they become available for download, regardless of whether you downloaded them.
  • No copy of your file is kept beyond this window.

Submitted Links

  • When you paste a link, EconverterS makes a server-side HTTP request to inspect the media title and verify it is a reachable, public file.
  • The full URL is used only to perform this check and to fetch the media for conversion. It is not retained in routine logs after the job ends.
  • Links to private networks, localhost, or cloud metadata endpoints are blocked and never fetched.
  • Links that require login, a paywall, or DRM are not fetched — an error is shown instead.

Download Privacy

  • Downloads are private to your browser session. No other user or browser session can access your converted file.
  • Once a download expires (after 30 minutes), the file is permanently deleted and the download link returns an error.

Cookies & Sessions

  • EconverterS sets one session cookie (econverters_sid) to associate your conversion jobs with your browser session. This cookie is HttpOnly and not accessible to JavaScript.
  • No third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies are used.
⚑ Needs review: Determine whether a cookie consent notice is required in your jurisdiction before public launch.

Logs

  • Server logs record event types (job started, job completed, job failed) and anonymous job IDs only.
  • Original filenames, submitted URLs, and session identifiers are not written to routine logs.

No Accounts, Tracking, or Advertising

EconverterS does not require an account, does not track you across sessions or websites, and does not display advertising.
